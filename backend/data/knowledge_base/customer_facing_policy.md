# SmartCoffee Pro: Customer Interaction Policy

## Support Channels
*   **Email Support:** help@smartcoffee.com
*   **Phone Support:** 1-800-555-0123 (Available 9 AM - 5 PM EST)
*   **Help Center:** [www.smartcoffee.com](http://www.smartcoffee.com)

## Customer Service Standards
*   **Response Time:** We aim to respond to all email inquiries within 24 business hours.
*   **Greeting Standard:** Our staff is trained to greet you within 5 seconds of entry with a smile and eye contact.
*   **Order Verification:** For accuracy, staff will always repeat your order back to you before finalizing payment.
*   **Feedback & Complaints:** We value your experience. All complaints are recorded in our digital logbook for management review to ensure continuous improvement.
